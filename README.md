# 🚧 Welcome to RoboRacing Micromouse's Hardware Repo 🚧
## What is Micromouse?
Micromouse is a new member program designed with the intention of providing an introduction to the electrical and firmware side of developing robots as a part of RoboRacing, while also preparing for a fun and competitive racing competition! Members will work on their own "mouse" that will be able to solve a 16x16 maze as quickly as possible.

## Our Mice
We made our first team mouse -- Ratatouilli, over the 2025-26 school year.
Ratatouilli competed in APEC in March 2026 at San Antonio!

This year, we are making *Ratatouillii*! 🐭🔥

## PCB project

The KiCad project is at [`pcb/micromouse-pcb.kicad_pro`](pcb/micromouse-pcb.kicad_pro). Its original component libraries are retained under `pcb/libraries/` and registered through project-specific library tables—no absolute paths or per-user library installation required.

See [`docs/kicad-library-guide.md`](docs/kicad-library-guide.md) for first-time setup and the required workflow for adding or changing symbols, footprints, and 3D models.
